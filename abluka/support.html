How to play

  • 7×7 board; Black moves first.
  • Each turn: move your pawn one square in any of 8 directions, then place a red block on any empty square.
  • A player who cannot move on their turn loses. If a move traps both sides, the player who made it wins the set.
  • Matches are best of 3 sets.
