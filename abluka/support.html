How to play

  • 7×7 board; Black moves first.
  • Each turn: move your pawn one square in any of 8 directions, then place a red block on any empty square.
  • A player who cannot move on their turn loses. If a move traps both sides, the player who made it wins the set.
  • Matches are best of 3 sets.

Game modes

  • Two Players on one device and Vs Computer (Easy/Medium/Hard): fully offline.
  • Play Online (Game Center): invite friends or auto-match with other Apple devices.
  • Cross-platform online (iPhone and Android, ABLUKA game server): Quick Match, Private Room with a 6-character code, and Practice vs Server Bot. Quick Match pairs you with the server bot if no player joins within about 10 seconds.

Privacy: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Cross-platform play sends only your typed nickname (3–16 characters) and your moves to the ABLUKA game server – see the privacy policy.
