How to play

  1. Black and white each have one pawn on a 7×7 board. Black moves first.
  2. On your turn, move your pawn one step in any of 8 directions to an empty adjacent square.
  3. Then place a red block on any empty square. Blocks are permanent.
  4. A player who can't move their pawn on their turn loses.
  5. If the last move traps both sides, the player who made it wins the set.
  6. Matches are best of 3 sets: first to 2 wins.
